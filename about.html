About
Us

Emzandglam is a Rzeszow-based beauty brand founded in 2023 by Emmaculate Munashe Songore. We specialize in elegant, personalized makeup services for weddings, graduations, events, and everyday glam moments. Whether you're looking for a flawless bridal look or a glow-up for a casual outing, Emzandglam brings the magic to your location, at your convenience.

Our Vision

To be the leading beauty brand recognized for elegance, personalization, and convenience in makeup services.

Our Mission

To provide flawless, personalized makeup experiences that enhance confidence and celebrate every special moment.

Our Values

Precision & Craftsmanship

At Emzandglam, every brushstroke, contour, and highlight is crafted with careful precision. We believe makeup is an art form, and our commitment to detail ensures that every look is flawless, timeless, and tailored uniquely to you. From the softest glam to the boldest transformations, we bring expert artistry to every face.

Natural Beauty & Skin Safety

We are passionate about enhancing your natural beauty while prioritizing skin health. That’s why we exclusively use safe, high-quality, and skin-friendly products that nourish and protect. At Emzandglam, looking glamorous never means compromising your skin’s wellness.

Personalized Experience

Every client is unique—and so is every glam session. We take the time to understand your style, your needs, and your vision to create a makeup look that feels authentically you. Whether you want soft elegance or show-stopping drama, we customize your experience to celebrate your individuality.

Empowerment Through Glam

At Emzandglam, makeup is more than beauty—it’s empowerment. Our mission is to help you see and feel your own magic through every look we create. When you leave our chair, we want you to feel confident, radiant, and ready to own every room you walk into.
